Acoustic Biomarker Mathematical Formulas & Clinical Meanings

1. Pitch Period Entropy (PPE) (Little et al. 2009)

Measures impaired pitch control and micro-tremor on logarithmic semitone transformations: $r_i = 12 \log_2(f_{0,i} / f_{\text{median}})$. Shannon entropy of $r_i$ reveals involuntary vocal drift.

2. Jitter & Shimmer Perturbations

Jitter quantifies cycle-to-cycle frequency variations: $\frac{1}{N-1}\sum |T_i - T_{i+1}| / \bar{T}$. Shimmer quantifies cycle-to-cycle amplitude perturbations: $\frac{1}{N-1}\sum |A_i - A_{i+1}| / \bar{A}$.

3. Harmonics-to-Noise Ratio (HNR)

Measures the ratio of harmonic vocal energy to turbulent aspiration noise: $10 \log_{10}(R_{xx}(\tau) / (R_{xx}(0) - R_{xx}(\tau)))$. Normal voices show HNR > 20 dB; breathy dysphonia shows low HNR.

4. Recurrence Period Density Entropy (RPDE)

Nonlinear dynamical feature that embeds speech into $m$-dimensional phase space and calculates entropy of recurrence return times $T$, quantifying vocal tract turbulence.

5. Detrended Fluctuation Analysis (DFA)

Computes the fractal scaling exponent $\alpha$ via log-log regression of root-mean-square fluctuations $F(n)$ across box sizes $n$, sensitive to long-range self-similarity loss.

6. Bayesian Online Changepoint Detection (BOCPD)

Calculates the recursive posterior distribution of run-length $r_t$ under a Student-t conjugate prior, enabling instant detection of medication wear-off without fixed thresholds.
